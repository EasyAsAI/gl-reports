GL Reports privacy policy

GL Landscape & Irrigation LLC · Last updated October 13, 2026

GL Reports is a private tool of GL Landscape & Irrigation LLC, used only by people GL has connected to it. Messages from anyone else are ignored and never stored or read.

What it handles

What connected people send it (texts, voice notes and photos) passes through Telegram, a private server GL rents, and an AI service that reads it (set so the AI companies don't train on it), and is saved in GL's own Google Sheet and Google Drive.

Google data

GL Reports signs in to GL's own Google account with permission to use only the Google Drive files it creates itself: one Google Sheet and its photo folders. It cannot see any other files in that Drive. It uses this access only to save and update the reports people send it. It does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone beyond the services named above, which it uses only to run GL Reports.

Your choices

To see or remove what you've sent, contact GL Landscape & Irrigation directly.
